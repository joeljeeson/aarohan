About Aarohan

Aarohan is a signature tech & creativity festival at Benedict College of Engineering and Technology. It brings students, makers, and thinkers together for hands-on competitions, inspiring talks, and collaborative workshops that turn ideas into working prototypes.

Over two days the festival showcases robotics, hackathons, design challenges, and industry-led sessions — all focused on learning by doing, teamwork, and building projects that matter.

  • Hands-on workshops
  • Live hackathons & competitions
  • Speaker sessions & mentorship

A deeper look at Aarohan

Aarohan focuses on hands-on experience: teams form to solve real problems, mentors guide short sprints, and participants demo working prototypes. Judging rewards creativity, usability, and technical depth.

If you'd like, submit your project idea during registration — mentors may help refine it into a competition-ready entry.
